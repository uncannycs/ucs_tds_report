Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the TDS Reports module.

1. Why should we use this TDS Report module over standard Odoo accounting reports?

Standard Odoo accounting reports do not provide out-of-the-box compliance tracking for Indian Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) and Tax Collected at Source (TCS). This module bridges that gap by automatically categorizing posted journal entries into statutory tax sections (e.g., 194C, 194J, 194H, 194Q, 206C), aggregating base invoice values and tax amounts, pulling partner PAN numbers, and generating audit-ready multi-tab Excel workbooks customized for quarterly Form 26Q and 27EQ e-filing.

2. How does the module extract statutory Indian TDS tax sections automatically?

The module uses an intelligent Regular Expression (Regex) parser that scans journal line descriptions, tax record titles, and account names of posted accounting entries. When it detects standard section identifiers (e.g., "194C - Contractors", "194J - Professional Fees", "194Q - Purchase of Goods"), it automatically maps the line to the corresponding statutory section without requiring tedious manual tag entry on individual vendor bills.

3. Can I select multiple TDS and TCS liability accounts simultaneously?

Yes! The wizard menu (Accounting > Reporting > TDS Report) features a flexible multi-select account field. You can select specific TDS vendor deduction accounts, TCS customer collection accounts, or run a comprehensive report across all tax withholding liability accounts defined in your Chart of Accounts simultaneously.

4. Does the generated Excel workbook separate data into monthly worksheets?

Yes! The exported Excel (.xlsx) file is structured for maximum clarity and compliance:
• Summary Sheet: Presents consolidated section-wise totals, overall base amounts, total tax withheld, and partner count.
• Monthly Sheets: Automatically creates separate tab sheets for each month within your selected date range (e.g., April 2026, May 2026, June 2026), complete with monthly partner sub-totals.

5. Where are customer and vendor PAN numbers pulled from for compliance?

PAN numbers are dynamically fetched from the pan_no or vat field on the associated res.partner master record of each transaction line. If a partner record lacks a registered PAN, the report clearly highlights the cell with "PAN NOT AVAILABLE", allowing your finance team to identify missing tax details prior to filing quarterly returns.

6. Can we customize the date range for quarterly, monthly, or annual reporting?

Absolutely. The reporting wizard allows you to define custom Start Date and End Date filters. You can generate reports for a single calendar month, a tax quarter (Q1: Apr-Jun, Q2: Jul-Sep, Q3: Oct-Dec, Q4: Jan-Mar), or the entire Indian Financial Year (Apr 1 to Mar 31) effortlessly.

7. What happens if no posted journal entries match the selected filters?

If no posted accounting entries match your selected date range and tax accounts, the wizard gracefully handles the scenario by displaying a clear Odoo warning notification ("No posted journal entries found for the selected period and accounts"). This prevents the generation of empty or corrupted Excel downloads.

8. Does this module require changes to our existing chart of accounts?

No configuration overhaul is required! The module works out-of-the-box with your existing Odoo Accounting setup. It inspects standard posted account.move.line entries across your existing journals and tax accounts without requiring custom database schema changes or account re-mapping.

9. Are unposted or draft journal entries included in TDS calculations?

No. To enforce strict financial compliance and prevent inaccurate reporting from unapproved transactions, the report engine strictly filters for posted entries (state = 'posted'). Draft, cancelled, or unposted journal vouchers are completely excluded from calculations.

10. How are multi-currency transactions calculated in the TDS report?

For vendor bills or customer invoices processed in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.), the module automatically converts all base invoice amounts and tax values into your company’s functional currency (INR). It utilizes the official historical exchange rate recorded on the date of journal entry posting for 100% currency accuracy.

11. Does the report support both Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) and Tax Collected at Source (TCS)?

Yes! The module handles both sides of Indian statutory tax withholding:
• TDS (Section 194 series): Tracks tax deducted on vendor payments and expense bills.
• TCS (Section 206C series): Tracks tax collected from customers on sales invoices (e.g., sale of scrap, timber, or high-value goods).

12. How are invoice base amounts and tax amounts broken down in the Excel sheet?

Each row in the detailed monthly worksheets provides an itemized breakdown including: Posting Date, Journal Entry Reference, Vendor / Customer Name, PAN Number, Tax Account & Extracted TDS Section, Gross Base Amount (Before Tax), Applied Tax Percentage (%), Tax Amount Deducted / Collected, and Net Payable / Receivable Balance.

13. Is the exported Excel file compatible with statutory tax filing tools?

Yes. The generated Excel workbook is formatted to align directly with Indian Income Tax Department (ITD) e-filing software and NSDL return preparation utilities. You can copy-paste or import data directly into government offline utilities for Form 26Q and Form 27EQ preparation.

14. Can accounting managers restrict access to TDS report generation?

Yes. Access to the TDS Report wizard under Accounting > Reporting is strictly controlled by standard Odoo security groups. Only users granted Advisor / Accounting Manager or Billing security roles can execute the report wizard and download financial tax exports.

15. Are partner-level sub-totals and grand totals included in the Excel export?

Yes! Every monthly worksheet includes automated, formula-driven sub-totals grouped by partner and tax section, as well as a formatted grand total row at the bottom of each tab sheet. This allows accountants to instantly reconcile report outputs against trial balances and ledger accounts.
